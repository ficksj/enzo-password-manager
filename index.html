GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Where is my vault stored?+

Your encrypted vault is saved in Enzo's local Windows application data directory. Enzo does not upload it to a server.

Can I use Enzo without Windows Hello?+

Yes. Windows Hello is optional. You can create and unlock your vault with your Enzo PIN.

Can I move my vault to another device?+

Encrypted backup and restore support is planned. Keep the current vault on its original device until backup support is available.

Is Enzo open source?+

Yes. Review the source code, report an issue, or contribute on GitHub.
